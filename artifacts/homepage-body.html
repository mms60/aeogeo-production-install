Four Connected Approaches to Modern Search

What Are AEO, GEO, AIO, and SEO?

People no longer rely on just one way to find businesses. They use Google, local search results, maps, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and other AI-powered tools. Each of these four optimization disciplines addresses a different part of how people discover information online.

For a deeper comparison, read our SEO vs. AEO vs. GEO vs. AIO guide.

01 · AEO

AEO: Answer Engine Optimization

Help your business become a useful answer to customer questions.

AEO focuses on creating clear, direct answers to the questions people ask. For example, when someone searches for a service, asks a question on Google, or uses a voice assistant, well-organized content can make it easier for search systems to identify relevant answers.

Learn What AEO Is

02 · GEO

GEO: Generative Engine Optimization

Help AI platforms understand and reference your business.

GEO focuses on how generative AI platforms discover, interpret, and reference information about your business. This includes clear business information, useful website content, supporting evidence, and consistent information across the internet.

Learn What GEO Is

03 · AIO

AIO: AI Optimization

Make your website easier for AI systems to access and understand.

AIO focuses on the technical side of AI readiness. This includes website structure, access for AI crawlers, structured data, and the way important information is presented on your website.

Read: What Is AI Search Optimization?

04 · SEO

SEO: Search Engine Optimization

Build a strong foundation in traditional and local search.

SEO helps your website appear in relevant search engine results. It includes useful content, website performance, page structure, internal links, and local business information.

What Business Owners Ask

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the questions business owners and marketers most frequently ask about Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), pricing, and our approach.

What is AI Search Optimization?

AI Search Optimization helps make your business easier for AI-powered search platforms to discover, understand, and reference. It involves improving your website content, technical structure, business information, and online presence. At AEOGEO Search, we bring together four disciplines: AIO (AI Optimization), GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), and SEO (Search Engine Optimization).

How is AI Search Optimization different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO focuses on helping your website appear in search engine results. AI Search Optimization builds on that foundation. It also focuses on helping AI platforms understand your business, find relevant information, and reference your website when generating answers. Both are important. Traditional SEO remains a key part of a complete search strategy.

What is GEO, and how is it different from AEO?

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization. It focuses on making your business information easier for generative AI platforms to understand and reference. AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization. It focuses on providing clear answers to specific questions. In simple terms, AEO helps your content answer questions. GEO helps AI systems understand the broader information and context about your business.

Can you guarantee that my business will appear in ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews?

No. No legitimate agency can guarantee that an AI platform will recommend your business or cite your website. AI-generated answers can change based on the question, location, available information, and platform updates. We focus on improving the factors within our control, including website accessibility, content quality, structured data, and consistent business information.

How long does AI Search Optimization take?

There is no universal timeline. Progress depends on your website, industry, competition, content, and how search engines and AI platforms discover and process information. We establish priorities, complete the agreed work, and monitor the indicators included in your selected plan.

Do I still need traditional SEO and Local SEO?

Yes. People still use traditional Google searches, maps, and local search results to find businesses. SEO helps establish your visibility in these channels, while AEO and GEO help prepare your content and business information for AI-powered search. These approaches work best as part of a connected strategy.

How do you measure AI search visibility?

We review several indicators, depending on your selected service. These may include whether your business appears in selected AI-generated answers, whether your website addresses important customer questions, whether your website is technically accessible to search systems, whether your business information is consistent online, and traditional organic search performance. Our reports explain what we observed and what improvements we recommend.

Do you work with businesses outside Texas?

Yes. AEOGEO Search is based in McKinney, Texas, but serves businesses throughout all 50 states. We work with local service providers, e-commerce businesses, B2B companies, SaaS providers, multi-location organizations, and in-house marketing teams.

What is the difference between the Free GEO Audit and the AI Visibility Snapshot?

They serve two different purposes. The Free GEO Website Audit scans accessible website pages and evaluates technical, content, and AI-readiness factors. It provides findings to help you identify improvements. The Free AI Visibility Snapshot is a self-assessment checklist that helps you consider your website's readiness for AI search. It does not scan your website or measure actual AI-generated mentions. Both can help you decide where to start.

Do you offer free DIY tools?

Yes. We offer tools to help you review website content, evaluate AI readiness, and configure crawler access. These include the GEO Content Checker, AI Content Humanizer, Robots.txt Generator, and other resources. Our tools are designed to help business owners and marketing professionals take a more active role in improving their online presence.

What is the difference between AI search and enterprise AI search?

Enterprise AI search usually refers to technology that helps employees or customers find information inside a company's private databases, documents, and internal systems. AEOGEO Search focuses on something different: your business's visibility in external AI-powered search platforms. We help businesses improve how their websites and brand information appear when people use tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI search. Our focus is helping potential customers discover your business.
