AI Crawler Accessibility Testing

If AI search crawlers cannot access your website's important content, they may have difficulty discovering and understanding what your business offers.

Our crawler accessibility audit examines how your website responds to representative AI crawler requests and identifies technical issues that may interfere with access.

What you can evaluate

  • AI crawler permissions: Review robots.txt rules affecting major AI and search crawlers.
  • Website accessibility: Identify HTTP errors, redirect problems, and access barriers.
  • Content availability: Determine whether important website content is present in the HTML returned to crawlers.
  • JavaScript dependencies: Identify pages that may rely heavily on client-side rendering to display important content.
  • Indexing directives: Check for noindex instructions and other directives that may affect discoverability.
  • Sitemap discovery: Examine sitemap availability and discoverable website pages.
  • Technical issues: Identify problems that could make your website more difficult to crawl and interpret.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this the same as your free robots.txt tool?

No. Our free robots.txt tool helps you create or review your robots.txt file. AI Visibility & Schema Studio goes further by evaluating website accessibility, testing representative crawler requests, examining website content, auditing structured data, and generating schema markup.

Does this guarantee my website will appear in ChatGPT or other AI search results?

No. No technical tool can guarantee that an AI platform will crawl, cite, recommend, or mention your business. This tool helps identify technical and structured-data opportunities that support website accessibility and machine understanding.

Does the crawler test verify actual visits from AI search engines?

The tool simulates representative crawler user-agent requests. These tests do not independently prove that a provider's actual crawler IP addresses can access your website. Server and CDN logs may be needed for that verification.

Can I use the generated schema immediately?

Generated markup should be reviewed before implementation. Confirm that all business information, page details, URLs, and other properties are accurate. The generated output is a starting point, not a substitute for final validation.

Do I need a developer?

You can review and copy generated JSON-LD yourself. Depending on your website platform and implementation, you may want a developer to install the markup and verify that it is working correctly.

Does schema guarantee better rankings?

No. Structured data helps search engines interpret information, but it does not guarantee rankings, rich results, or AI citations.
