What this free scan does and does not tell you

  • It reads the HTML the server actually returns. It does not run page JavaScript.
  • Dates come only from what a page declares: structured data, article metadata, a time element, or a visible "last updated" label.
  • Age alone is not decay. An old page with current information is not treated as stale; a page that declares no date is reported as undated, not as fresh.
  • It reports crawl facts only. Per-page scoring and specific fixes are part of the detailed report.
  • It cannot see pages that are blocked, broken, behind a login, or not linked from the site or its sitemap.
  • A scan can stop at the 10-page limit, at skipped or blocked pages, or at its time limit. The summary always reports how many discovered pages were read, skipped, and never reached, and says so when the result is partial. The discovered count is marked "at least" whenever the sitemap or unread pages could contain URLs the scan did not see.
