WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN SEO, AEO, AND GEO?

Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

SEO focuses on improving your website’s visibility in traditional search engine results. It includes technical optimization, keyword targeting, content development, website structure, and authority building.

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

AEO focuses on making your content easier for answer engines and search systems to identify, understand, and use when responding to customer questions.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

GEO focuses on improving your brand’s visibility and representation across generative AI platforms, helping your business become a relevant source for AI-generated answers and recommendations.

Why Do All Three Matter?

Your customers aren’t searching in just one place anymore. They may use Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, or other AI-powered platforms to research businesses and make purchasing decisions.

A comprehensive strategy considers traditional search visibility alongside emerging AI-powered discovery experiences.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ABOUT AI SEARCH VISIBILITY

What is an AI Search Visibility Assessment?

An AI Search Visibility Assessment evaluates factors that may influence how easily AI-powered search platforms can discover, understand, reference, and recommend your business. It helps identify potential opportunities to improve your website, content, and online authority.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO, and GEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) focuses on improving visibility in traditional search engine results.

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) focuses on structuring content so search engines and answer platforms can identify and present useful answers to questions.

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) focuses on improving the likelihood that generative AI platforms recognize, reference, and recommend your business in AI-generated responses.

These strategies overlap and work best together.

Does my Google ranking determine whether ChatGPT recommends my business?

No. Traditional Google rankings alone do not determine whether your business appears in ChatGPT responses. AI platforms use different retrieval systems and information sources. Your content, brand identity, reputation, website accessibility, and relevant third-party information can all contribute to your visibility.

Can my business appear in ChatGPT and Perplexity?

Yes. Businesses can be discovered, referenced, and cited in AI-generated answers. However, visibility varies by platform, question, location, available information, and other factors. No optimization provider can guarantee inclusion in every AI-generated response.

Is having a website enough to appear in AI search?

Having a website is an important starting point, but it doesn’t guarantee AI visibility. Your website needs accessible, useful, well-organized content that clearly communicates your business, expertise, products, and services.

How often should I update my website for AI search?

There is no universal monthly requirement, but regular content reviews and updates are valuable. Businesses should update outdated information, expand useful content, address new customer questions, and monitor changes in their competitive landscape.

Can small businesses benefit from AEO and GEO?

Absolutely. Local businesses, professional service providers, ecommerce companies, agencies, and B2B organizations can all benefit from improving how their brands are represented in AI-powered search experiences.

Does this assessment guarantee that my business will appear in AI search results?

No. The assessment identifies potential opportunities and areas for improvement. AI search platforms independently determine which businesses and sources they include in their responses. Optimization improves readiness and creates opportunities but cannot guarantee placement.

What should I do after receiving my assessment?

Start by addressing the most important gaps identified in your results. A professional AEO and GEO analysis can provide a deeper review of your website, content, brand authority, competitors, and AI search presence.
