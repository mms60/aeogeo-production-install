About AEOGEO Search

Helping Small Businesses Get Found in Google and AI Search

Search is changing. People still use Google, but they're also asking ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity which businesses to trust, what products to buy, and who can solve their problems.

Your business deserves to be part of those conversations.

AEOGEO Search helps businesses improve how they appear across traditional search engines and AI-powered platforms. We bring together Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), and AI Optimization (AIO) to help customers and search platforms better understand what your business offers.

Our goal is simple: make your business easier to find, understand, and trust.

  • Clearly defined monthly plans.
  • Published prices for individual services.
  • Custom quotes for specialized projects.
  • No long-term contracts on monthly managed plans.
  • Human-reviewed content and agreed deliverables.

Modern Search

Search Is Changing. Your Business Can Keep Up.

A website is still important, but simply having one isn't enough.

Your website needs to explain what you do, who you serve, what makes you different, and why customers should trust you. It also needs to make that information easy for search engines and AI platforms to access and understand.

That's where modern search optimization comes in.

We bring four connected disciplines together:

Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

Improve your website's ability to appear in traditional search results, including relevant local searches.

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

Organize your content so search engines and answer platforms can quickly identify useful answers to customer questions.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

Make your business information clearer and more useful to AI platforms that generate recommendations, summaries, and answers.

AI Optimization (AIO)

Improve the technical accessibility and structure of your website so AI systems can better access and interpret your content.

These disciplines work together. Our approach is designed to support your visibility wherever potential customers are searching.

Our Process

How We Work

We believe good optimization starts with understanding your business, not simply adding keywords to a page.

Our process focuses on useful content, clear information, and improvements that support real business goals.

  1. Step 1

    Understand Your Business

    We learn what you offer, who your customers are, what makes your business different, and what questions people ask before choosing a provider.

  2. Step 2

    Find the Opportunities

    We review your website, content, technical setup, and online presence to identify gaps and areas that may be holding you back.

  3. Step 3

    Improve What Matters

    We prioritize practical improvements. That may include rewriting website pages, creating helpful articles, improving structured data, or making your business information more consistent.

  4. Step 4

    Review and Refine

    Search platforms change, and customer questions evolve. We review progress, identify new opportunities, and adjust the work based on what we learn.
