The New Search Environment

What Is AI Search Tuning?

AI search tuning is simply taking the organization of your website, content, and data and enabling AI services (Google Gemini Claude Perplexity ChatGPT etc.) to find, understand, and cite your business as a direct answer. Instead of what we know as regular SEO by ranking for a set of 3 (blue) links to the website on a page of results - in AI search improvement you want to show up inside the answer itself. This form of search is a combination of four structures or pillars - AI improvement (AIO), Generative Engine Improvement (GEO), Answer Engine Improvement (AEO) and Search Engine Improvement (SEO). Find inside the paper Aggarwal et al. (2024), GEO: Generative Engine Improvement, ACM KDD 2024 to learn why and to see published (and now publicly available) what impact may be possible: we find some increases of 40% in visibility in generative engine responses. This does not guarantee you will see that... Or necessarily in the results of our work.

Search is changing. Per a February 2026 survey referenced in a Pew Research Center report published on June 17, 2026, 49% of U.S. adults are already using AI chatbots (in a February 2026 survey cited in this Pew Research Center report released June 17, 2026). Simply having one website is no longer sufficient.

Your products and services must describe what you do, who you serve, and why you can be trusted. You can take up even more of their screen time with AI answers. Ahrefs' 2026 study in February found that featuring with an AI Overview lowered the average clickthrough rate for its number one ranked page in a sample of 300,000 keywords by 58%. They based the findings on December 2023 and December 2025 and did not record the percentage of uncredited pages that would have lost clicks.

Simple is what we do. We make your business easier to find, easier to understand, and more likely to be referred to.
