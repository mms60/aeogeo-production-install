Three connected disciplines

What Are SEO, AEO and GEO?

These three approaches work together to help customers find your business through traditional search engines and newer AI-powered discovery platforms.

SEO

Search Engine Optimization

SEO helps your website appear in traditional search engine results, including Google and Bing.

It involves improving your website content, keywords, technical performance, page structure, internal links and online authority.

The goal: Help potential customers find your website when they search for the products and services you offer.

See how traditional SEO fits into AI search

AEO

Answer Engine Optimization

AEO focuses on making your website content easy for search engines and AI-powered answer engines to understand and use when responding to questions.

This means providing clear answers, organizing information logically, addressing common customer questions and using structured content.

The goal: Make your business and expertise easier to understand when customers ask questions online. These answer engine optimization tools start with clear, useful pages.

Learn what AEO is

GEO

Generative Engine Optimization

GEO helps improve how your business, website and brand information can be discovered, understood and referenced by generative AI platforms.

This includes improving your business information, website content, structured data, credibility signals and relationships between your brand, services and areas of expertise.

The goal: Improve your chances of being recognized and referenced when AI platforms generate recommendations and answers.

Learn what GEO is

Why all three matter

Why Do Businesses Need All Three?

Customers don't all search the same way.

Your customers search in different places

One person might search Google for a local service provider. Another might ask ChatGPT to recommend the best companies in their area. Someone else might use Perplexity to compare businesses before making a purchase.

A strong online visibility strategy considers all these discovery methods.

The three approaches work together

SEO helps customers find your website. AEO helps your content answer their questions. GEO helps AI systems better understand your business and its relevance.

They are complementary strategies, not competing marketing approaches.

Free tool questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Free SEO, AEO and GEO Tools

Quick answers about the free tools, AI search visibility and what you can reasonably expect from a self-serve audit or checklist.

What are the best free SEO tools for small businesses?

The best free SEO tools depend on what you want to accomplish. Website audits help identify technical and content issues, content checkers help improve website pages, and visibility assessments help you understand how clearly your business communicates its services online. AEOGEO Search provides several free SEO tools to help small businesses get started without committing to a monthly agency service.

Are there free AEO tools for ChatGPT and other AI search platforms?

Yes. Free AEO and GEO tools can help evaluate website content, technical accessibility, structured information and other signals that support AI discovery. AEOGEO Search offers the GEO Website Audit, GEO Content Checker and AI Visibility Snapshot to help businesses assess their readiness. These tools do not guarantee that ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Perplexity will mention your business.

How can I make my website appear in ChatGPT search results?

Start by making sure your website is accessible to relevant crawlers, your business information is accurate, and your content clearly explains your products, services and expertise. Create useful pages that answer the questions potential customers ask. Support important business information with appropriate structured data and trustworthy references. There is no guaranteed method of securing an AI-generated recommendation, but improving these foundations can make your website easier to understand and evaluate.

What is the difference between an SEO audit and a GEO audit?

An SEO audit generally examines how well a website supports traditional search engine crawling, indexing, rankings and organic traffic. A GEO audit also considers how clearly website content, business information, technical accessibility and trust signals communicate information relevant to generative AI systems. The two approaches overlap, and a strong website should consider both. You can start with our free GEO Website Audit or read the SEO, AEO, GEO and AIO comparison.

Are free SEO tools enough to improve my website?

Free tools can help identify problems and provide a starting point for improvements. However, larger websites and competitive industries often require deeper keyword research, competitor analysis, ongoing performance tracking and technical implementation. AEOGEO Search offers professional services when you need help beyond the free tools.

Do I need to pay for AI search optimization?

No. You can make many useful website improvements yourself using free tools and publicly available resources. Professional research, comprehensive reporting, advanced analysis and implementation require additional time and expertise. AEOGEO Search offers both free tools and professional services, so you can choose the level of support that fits your business.

Can AI search optimization replace traditional SEO?

No. AEO and GEO build on traditional SEO rather than replace it. Search engines remain important sources of information for AI-powered search experiences. Technical accessibility, useful content, relevant keywords and online authority continue to matter. A combined SEO, AEO and GEO strategy helps businesses prepare for both traditional and AI-powered discovery.

Are the tools on this page really free?

Yes. The GEO Website Audit, GEO Content Checker, AI Visibility Snapshot, AI Content Humanizer, Content Waste Quiz and Robots.txt Generator are free to use. No paid product is required to run them.
