Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what AEOGEO Search collects and how we use it.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

1. Who We Are

AEOGEO Search provides AI Optimization (AIO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), and Search Engine Optimization (SEO), along with the related services described on our Pricing page. We are based in McKinney, TX 75072 and work with clients nationwide.

Contact AEOGEO Search at aeogeosearch@atomicmail.io or (469) 905-2836. The location above is a city, state, and ZIP code, not a published street or walk-in address. This policy applies to the website and services we provide.

2. Information We Collect

You may provide your name, email address, phone number, website address, business information, and messages when you contact us, create an account, or order a service. Do not submit confidential credentials or sensitive personal information through the consultation form.

Hosting and security providers may process request logs such as IP addresses, timestamps, browser and device details, and error information. Account and payment providers process information needed to authenticate you, manage a subscription, or complete a transaction.

Our free Snapshot checklist and Robots Text Generator process the information you enter in your browser. They do not themselves scan your website or submit a consultation. Downloading or copying generated rules does not install them on your website.

The optional URL mode in the Content Checker sends the public page URL to Jina AI Reader only after you consent and run a check. The provider retrieves that public page. Do not submit confidential, account-only or token-bearing URLs. Pasted text is analyzed locally by this checker and is not sent to the reader. This checker does not intentionally store your input or add it to analytics.

For content orders, we store the website domain, customer email, submitted brief, payment references, private drafts and approved content in our service database. Stripe processes payment details. We do not store card details. Owner notices use our existing Netlify form-notification channel.

Private content-order and review pages use signed browser cookies and scoped access links. Treat these links as confidential and do not forward an owner review link. Guest content orders do not create an audit account or grant audit access.

3. Uses and Service Providers

We use information to answer inquiries, deliver the agreed services, administer accounts and billing, keep appropriate business records, and maintain security. We do not sell personal information.

The website uses Netlify for hosting and contact-form handling, Supabase for account authentication and related application data, and Stripe for payment and subscription billing. These providers receive the information necessary for their functions. Card details are entered with Stripe, not in our consultation form.

The website requests fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may send technical request information to that provider. Links to external reports or AI platforms are separate websites with their own privacy policies.

4. Cookies, Browser Storage and Analytics

The website build reviewed on October 30, 2026 does not include an optional third-party visitor-analytics platform, advertising pixel, or session-replay tracker. We have not named an analytics service that is not installed. Server-side hosting logs are separate from browser analytics.

Necessary account functionality may store authentication/session information in browser storage. Stripe may use cookies or similar technologies on its payment and billing pages for functionality, security, and fraud prevention. You can manage browser storage in your browser settings; blocking necessary storage may prevent sign-in or billing features from working.

No optional tracking consent banner is triggered by this build because no optional tracker is installed. If optional tracking is added, this notice and any required consent or opt-out controls must be updated before that tracking starts.

5. Security and Retention

We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We keep personal information for the purposes described here, as needed for service delivery, legitimate business records, legal obligations, disputes, and security.

6. Your Choices

You may contact aeogeosearch@atomicmail.io or (469) 905-2836 to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to ask a privacy question. We may need information to verify your identity before responding. We will not ask for your password. You do not need to create an account to make a request.

7. Texas Privacy Rights

Texas residents may have rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, where the Act applies, to confirm processing and access personal data, correct inaccuracies, delete data, obtain a portable copy, and opt out of targeted advertising, sales of personal data, or certain consequential profiling. Applicable exceptions may limit these rights. The Act generally exempts qualifying small businesses, with a specific rule requiring consent before a small business sells sensitive personal data. Serving clients nationwide does not by itself determine whether the Act applies.

Submit a request by email to aeogeosearch@atomicmail.io with the subject Texas Privacy Request, or call (469) 905-2836. Where the Act applies, we will respond within 45 days, with a further 45 days when reasonably necessary and with notice during the initial period. We will explain a denial and how to appeal.

To appeal, reply to the denial email with Texas Privacy Appeal and explain your concern. Where the Act applies, we will respond in writing within 60 days and explain the outcome. You may also contact the Texas Attorney General. We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

8. Third-Party Platforms

Our services may discuss Google Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and ChatGPT. These services are operated independently. Review their own policies before sending information to them. A reference to a platform does not imply affiliation or endorsement.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided such information.

10. Changes and Contact

We update the date on this page when its content changes. Questions may be sent to AEOGEO Search, McKinney, TX 75072, aeogeosearch@atomicmail.io, or (469) 905-2836.
