Questions, answered straight

How exactly do I get the file after I pay?

Stripe sends you to our thank-you page. The moment you arrive, the download starts (and a button re-offers it). Keep the .docx file. Your Stripe receipt emails as usual; everything else, including the file, lives on the thank-you page.

What format is the kit, and how long is it?

One Word document (.docx), editable in Microsoft Word and Google Docs, about 30 pages. Print it for your ops binder or convert to PDF for a client hand-off. No login, no account, no renewal.

Do my listings actually rank if I buy this?

No one can honestly sell that guarantee, and we do not pretend to. What this kit does guarantee: your business will show up with one consistent, verifiable identity everywhere it matters, which is the prerequisite every AI system and directory needs before it can trust you at all. Results from visibility you will see yourself; what we sell is the clean, repeatable work.

Directories change constantly. Will this stay accurate?

The instructions were written against the November 2026 flows, including moves like Bing's relocation to bing.com/forbusiness and Apple's current verification paths. When a directory changes materially, buyers of this release get the corrected section by email at no charge.

Which industries does it fit?

Any U.S. business with customers in a defined geography or industry: home services, medical and dental, legal, auto, restaurants, hospitality, beauty, real estate, B2B services. One walkthrough covers choosing your vertical directory (your #1 trade directory for your industry, spelled out with criteria), so the kit works no matter your category.

Can my agency use it for clients?

Yes. One purchase covers every business and client campaign you run, forever. The one restriction: do not redistribute or resell the kit itself, or include it in a competing product.

All sales final? Really?

Really. It is an instant digital download, unbreakably tied to one purchase moment.
