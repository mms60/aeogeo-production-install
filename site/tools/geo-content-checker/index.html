    Content checker and service questions

    What does this free content checker do?

    It identifies possible content needs from English-language editorial cues in one public page or pasted text. It does not provide an outline, rewrite or implementation guide.

    Does a checklist result prove my page is ready for AI citations?

    No. The implementation score is a heuristic count of detected editorial requirements, not a ranking prediction, citation certification, fact check, plagiarism check or competitor analysis. Extracted navigation and other page text may affect the observations.

    What do the content options cost?

    Individual page content is $259 per page. The monthly option is $1,000 per month for four articles. Each order or subscription covers one domain. Ordering stays closed until all safeguards are verified.

    Is there human review before delivery?

    Yes. The service requires human review and approval before finished content can be released as a download. Unreviewed drafts must remain private. Human review does not guarantee that every fact, citation or outcome is correct.

    When can paid production begin?

    Only after full payment has been verified by the server. An inquiry, a selected plan, a checkout redirect or creation of a subscription is not proof of payment. No payment or production starts from this checker.

    Can I buy without an audit account?

    The content service is separate from audit accounts and audit subscriptions. No audit purchase or sign-in is needed to use the checker. Content ordering is kept closed until its safeguards are verified.

    What happens to the URL or text I enter?

    URL mode sends the public page URL to Jina AI Reader after you consent and click Check my page. The reader can access that public page. Do not submit private, confidential or token-bearing URLs. Pasted text is analyzed locally by this checker and is not sent to the reader. The tool does not intentionally save your input or add it to analytics.

    How will monthly cancellation work?

    Cancel before renewal to stop the next charge. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period and does not automatically refund that period. Scope, delivery and any written exceptions must be agreed before ordering opens.
