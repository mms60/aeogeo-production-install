The AEOGEO Search Framework

What Is AEOM?

The AI Engine Optimization Method. Our systematic approach to getting your business found, understood, and recommended.

AEOM, the AI Engine Optimization Method, is the framework AEOGEO Search uses to prepare businesses for the age of AI-powered search. It combines AI Optimization (AIO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), and Search Engine Optimization (SEO) into one repeatable process.

AEOM organizes content and website improvements into a connected process. Research informs the work, but it does not prove that our branded method guarantees a particular result. Research by Aggarwal et al. (2024), GEO: Generative Engine Optimization, ACM KDD 2024 reported visibility improvements of up to 40% in generative engine responses in its experiments, with results varying by method and domain.

How It Works

How Does AEOM Work?

Discover, Optimize, Amplify, and Monitor organize the agreed work: review your business and customer questions, improve content and structure, create useful material, and review the available indicators. We document measured results and limitations rather than promise rankings, citations, traffic, or leads.

01

Discover

We start by understanding your business, your audience, and the questions potential customers ask AI platforms before choosing a provider.

02

Optimize

We improve your website content, structure, and relevance so both search engines and AI systems clearly understand what you do and why you should be trusted.

03

Amplify

We develop useful website articles and social content that communicate your expertise, answer customer questions, and reinforce your brand message.

04

Monitor

We review agreed visibility indicators, document measured results and limitations, and guide next steps. Rankings and citations are not guaranteed.
