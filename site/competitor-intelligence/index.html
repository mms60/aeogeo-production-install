Residential solar · Austin, TX · Prepared as an illustrative preview

Sample data
Two kinds of competitorThey overlap, but they are not the same.
Commercial competitorsBusinesses selling the same product or service to the same customers.
Search competitorsSites appearing for the same searches—directories, manufacturers, national lead-generation sites and publishers—even when they do not sell the same thing.

A roofing company may compete with other roofers commercially, but its search results can also include directories, manufacturers, national lead-generation sites and informational publishers. This sample maps three commercial competitors and two search competitors.

Competitors mapped53 commercial · 2 search

How to read the sample: All figures are illustrative. The report observes public pages and files, including site structure, metadata, robots.txt and sitemap access, and content coverage. Visibility and overlap scores are calculated directional signals from those public signals; search gaps and quick wins are analysis counts and prioritizations. These are not traffic, rankings, revenue or market share.
