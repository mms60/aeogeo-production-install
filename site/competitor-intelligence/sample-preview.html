Two kinds of competitor

Commercial competitors sell the same product or service to the same customers. Search competitors are directories, manufacturers, national lead-generation sites and publishers that appear for the same searches—even if they do not sell the same thing.

A roofing company may compete with other roofers commercially while also appearing alongside directories, manufacturers and national lead-generation sites in search. This sample maps three commercial competitors and two search competitors.

1. Executive Snapshot

BrightPath Solar shows a calculated readiness index of 72/100 across public service and educational pages, trailing regional leader SunPeak Energy (84/100) while ahead of HelioHome (58/100) and Lone Star Solar (43/100). The 0–100 index is a directional signal built from public search-readiness signals—crawl access, site structure, metadata, content coverage and answer-friendly formatting—not rankings, traffic or market share. Financing transparency and educational guides are core strengths; high-intent battery backup, outage calculator, and tax-credit comparison clusters represent immediate search and AI-citation opportunities.

3. Audience, 4 Ps & Search Gap Highlights

The 4 Ps, explained

  • Product — what competitors sell: Rivals bundle battery backup and roof-warranty guarantees prominently on primary landing pages.
  • Price — how they charge: HelioHome publishes tiered package pricing; SunPeak leads with monthly payment calculators before quote capture.
  • Place — where and how they reach customers: Austin + surrounding Central Texas suburbs dominate local landing pages across top rivals.
  • Promotion — how they market themselves: Rebate checklists and utility-bill estimators drive primary rival conversions.

SWOT & Priority Next Steps

  • Publish dedicated Austin utility-rebate and battery-backup comparison guides with FAQPage JSON-LD schema.
  • Pair transparent financing examples with clear warranty terms above the fold on core installation pages.
  • Add side-by-side equipment and service-scope comparisons to differentiate against quote-only competitors.
