Frequently Asked Questions About Pricing

Do I need a monthly plan to use your services?

No. You can buy an individual service, a fixed-scope project, or a one-time package without a monthly plan. Monthly support is available for businesses that want ongoing content, schema, and visibility work.

Can I buy just the Content Decay report without a plan?

Yes. The Content Decay report is $49 one time and needs no monthly plan. You scan up to 10 public pages free, then the report shows which pages declare no update date, have gone stale or are too thin for AI search, with a 0–100 score per page. Open the Content Decay report.

What's the difference between managed services and DIY tools?

With managed services, AEOGEO Search performs the agreed work. With DIY tools, you receive reports, recommendations, or templates and make the changes yourself or through your developer.

Are there long-term contracts?

Our monthly managed plans do not require long-term contracts. Subscriptions renew monthly until canceled. Please review the applicable billing terms before purchasing.

Can I change plans later?

You can contact us to discuss changing your managed service plan as your business needs evolve. Any changes will be confirmed before they take effect.

Are content services included in monthly plans?

Yes, depending on the selected plan. Growth includes one new article per month, while Authority includes two. Starter does not include new article production. Additional standalone content services are priced separately.

Do you guarantee search rankings or AI mentions?

No. Search engines and AI platforms control their own rankings and generated answers. We improve your website, content, and online information using established optimization practices, but cannot guarantee rankings, citations, traffic, or leads.

What happens after I request a quote?

We review your requirements, clarify the work involved, and provide a proposed scope and price. No payment is authorized simply by requesting a quote.

Can I get help choosing a service?

Yes. Tell us about your business, your website, and what you want to accomplish. We can recommend a practical starting point based on your needs and budget.
