Want to Know How Your Business Appears in AI-Powered Search?

Looking to improve your website content, strengthen your SEO, or build a more consistent digital presence? AEOGEO Search can help you identify opportunities and determine the right next steps.

Two Ways to Get Started

1. Do-It-Yourself Tools & Guides

If you or your developer prefer to make the changes yourselves, explore our self-serve options:

  • Free Snapshot Checklist

    An immediate, self-reported checklist to assess website readiness.

    Note: this is a self-assessment, not an automated website scan, and it does not test AI platforms directly.

  • Robots.txt Generator

    Optimize crawler accessibility.

  • Free Knowledge Library

    Guides and resources for ongoing optimization.

2. Request a Professional Consultation

Ready for expert support? Tell us about your goals, and let our team handle the strategy. We can help you explore:

  • AI Search Visibility

    Understand how your business appears in relevant AI-generated answers across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google Gemini.

  • GEO & AI Optimization

    Optimize your content specifically for AI-powered discovery.

  • SEO & Website Content

    Refresh existing pages and create useful new content that customers actually need.

  • Social Media Content

    Build a consistent content presence across your agreed social channels.

  • Ongoing Optimization

    Establish a repeatable process for monitoring and improving your digital presence.

What Happens Next?

  1. 1. We review your message within one business day.
  2. 2. We evaluate the support you requested and prepare for our conversation.
  3. 3. If useful, we arrange an optional consultation to discuss your specific business goals.

Submitting the contact form is completely optional and does not start an automated scan, create a purchase, or obligate you to buy services.
