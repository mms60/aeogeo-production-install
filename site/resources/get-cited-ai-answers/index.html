How to Get Cited in AI Answers: 12 GEO and AEO Strategies to Improve AI Search Visibility

By AEOGEO Search

Reviewed and updated November 24, 2026

Quick answer

Getting cited in AI answers means having your website, business, research, or published content referenced as a source in an AI-generated response. Neither GEO nor AEO guarantees citations, but businesses improve their opportunities by publishing authoritative content, providing verifiable information, establishing brand authority, and keeping their website technically accessible.

Getting your business cited in AI-generated answers is becoming an important part of modern search engine optimization. As people increasingly use ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, and Perplexity to research products, compare services, and find businesses, appearing in traditional search results is no longer the only way to reach potential customers.

Businesses now need to consider how their websites, content, expertise, and brand information are discovered and referenced by AI-powered search platforms.

This is where Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) come into play.

GEO focuses on improving a brand's visibility, mentions, and citations in generative AI responses. AEO focuses on making website content easy for search engines and answer engines to understand, extract, and use when responding to questions.

While neither discipline guarantees AI citations, businesses can improve their opportunities by publishing authoritative content, providing verifiable information, establishing brand authority, and maintaining a technically accessible website.

In this guide, we'll explain how AI citations work and share 12 practical strategies to help your business become a more visible and credible source in AI-generated answers.

What Does It Mean to Get Cited in AI Answers?

Getting cited in AI answers means having your website, business, research, or published content referenced as a source in an AI-generated response.

For example, someone might ask ChatGPT:

"Who are the best digital marketing agencies specializing in local SEO?"

The platform may generate a response identifying several agencies and provide links to websites or other sources supporting its recommendations.

If your company appears in that response or its supporting citations, you have earned AI search visibility.

AI citations can take several forms:

  • Website citations: An AI platform links directly to an article, service page, or research resource.
  • Brand mentions: Your company is named in an AI-generated answer.
  • Expert citations: Your research, quotations, or professional commentary are referenced.
  • Product recommendations: Your products or services appear in AI-generated comparisons.
  • Local recommendations: Your business is included when users ask for nearby providers or local service recommendations.

These outcomes are related but different. A brand mention does not necessarily mean your website was cited, and a website citation does not always produce a recommendation.

The goal of GEO is to improve your visibility across these different discovery opportunities.

How Do AI Search Engines Decide Which Websites to Cite?

AI search engines use different technologies and information sources to generate answers. Depending on the platform and query, they may retrieve information from search indexes, identify relevant documents, evaluate supporting information, and generate responses using multiple sources.

A simplified version of this process includes five stages:

  1. Query interpretation: Understanding the user's question and intent.
  2. Information retrieval: Finding relevant websites and other information sources.
  3. Content evaluation: Assessing the relevance and usefulness of retrieved information.
  4. Answer generation: Combining information into a conversational response.
  5. Source attribution: Providing references or links to supporting sources when available.

Google's AI search experiences, for example, use techniques that build on its existing search systems to identify relevant information and provide supporting links.

This means traditional SEO remains important. Google recommends continuing to follow its established technical SEO and helpful content practices rather than relying on special AI-specific markup.

Source: Google Search Central: AI Features and Your Website.

For businesses, the practical takeaway is straightforward: create content that is discoverable, relevant, accurate, useful, and supported by credible information. Our guide to AI search optimization covers how these retrieval systems differ from traditional rankings.

12 Strategies to Get Cited in AI Answers

1. Publish Original Statistics and Research

Original research is one of the strongest ways to differentiate your website from competitors.

AI systems have access to enormous amounts of repetitive information. Publishing unique statistics, research findings, surveys, and industry data gives your website something distinctive to contribute.

Consider publishing:

  • Original industry surveys.
  • Customer research findings.
  • Market analysis.
  • Product comparisons.
  • Performance benchmarks.
  • Industry trends.
  • Case studies with measurable results.

For example, a marketing agency could publish an annual report examining how local businesses are performing in AI search.

The report might include original data about brand mentions, AI citations, competitor visibility, and the types of websites referenced by different AI platforms.

Make your findings easy to understand. Include the research methodology, sample size, dates, definitions, and limitations.

When other websites reference your findings, you can also earn valuable third-party mentions and backlinks.

2. Include Expert Quotes and Professional Insights

AI-friendly content should demonstrate more than general knowledge.

It should incorporate professional experience and perspectives that help distinguish your information from generic articles.

Consider adding:

  • Quotations from subject-matter experts.
  • Professional recommendations.
  • Industry-specific observations.
  • Firsthand experience.
  • Expert commentary on emerging trends.
  • Practical examples from completed projects.

For example, instead of publishing another generic article about website optimization, include commentary from an SEO professional explaining how technical accessibility affects content discovery.

Identify the expert by name and include their relevant qualifications or professional background.

Expert contributions make content more useful to readers and provide additional context about the people behind the information.

3. Cite Authoritative Sources

Credible references help readers verify information and understand where important claims originate.

When publishing research-based content, link directly to reliable primary sources whenever possible.

Good sources include:

  • Government publications.
  • Academic research.
  • Industry studies.
  • Professional associations.
  • Official technical documentation.
  • Original survey data.
  • Recognized research organizations.

For example, an article discussing generative engine optimization could reference the 2024 research paper "GEO: Generative Engine Optimization," published in the proceedings of the ACM SIGKDD Conference.

The study examined different strategies for improving visibility in generative search engines.

Research citation: GEO: Generative Engine Optimization.

Avoid making unsupported claims or linking to secondary articles when the original research is available.

The objective is to create content that is transparent, verifiable, and useful.

4. Structure Content Around Questions and Direct Answers

Answer Engine Optimization starts with understanding what your audience wants to know.

People increasingly use conversational queries instead of entering short keyword phrases.

For example:

  • What is generative engine optimization?
  • How do I get my website cited by ChatGPT?
  • How can local businesses appear in AI search results?
  • Does schema markup help with AI visibility?
  • How do I measure AI search performance?

Create dedicated sections that answer these questions.

A useful content structure includes:

  1. A descriptive question-based heading.
  2. A concise answer immediately below the heading.
  3. A more detailed explanation.
  4. Supporting facts and examples.
  5. Relevant internal and external links.

For example:

How do you improve visibility in ChatGPT?

Businesses can improve their opportunities for visibility in ChatGPT by publishing authoritative content, maintaining an accessible website, establishing consistent brand information, earning relevant third-party mentions, and answering questions that align with customer needs. AI recommendations and citations are not guaranteed, and results can vary depending on the query and platform.

Follow the direct answer with practical steps and supporting information.

This structure makes the content easier for people to navigate and easier for search systems to interpret. Our AEO guide breaks down the full extraction pattern.

One article rarely establishes comprehensive expertise in a competitive subject.

Instead, build a connected library of resources covering the important questions, challenges, and topics within your industry.

A comprehensive content network helps establish the website's subject-matter focus and provides readers with additional information.

Avoid publishing repetitive articles that target slightly different keyword variations without adding meaningful value.

Topical authority comes from depth, relevance, and quality rather than simply publishing more pages.

6. Establish Consistent Brand and Entity Information

AI systems need to interpret the relationship between businesses, people, products, services, and other entities.

Your website should make it clear who your business is, what it does, who it serves, and where it operates.

Maintain consistent information across your digital presence.

Important business information includes:

  • Official business name.
  • Website URL.
  • Company description.
  • Business address or service area.
  • Contact information.
  • Primary services.
  • Industry specialization.
  • Company leadership.
  • Professional qualifications.
  • Relevant business profiles.

Use consistent branding across your website, social profiles, business directories, and professional listings.

For example, if your business specializes in commercial roofing, your website should clearly describe that specialization. Relevant project examples, professional credentials, and independent references can reinforce that positioning.

Do not rely on keyword repetition to establish brand authority.

Build a recognizable digital identity supported by accurate information.

7. Optimize Content for Readability and Information Extraction

AI search visibility is not just about what information you publish. It also matters how you organize it.

Make important information easy to locate and understand.

Recommended formatting practices include:

  • Use descriptive H1, H2, and H3 headings.
  • Answer important questions near the beginning of each section.
  • Use bullet points for lists.
  • Use numbered steps for processes.
  • Include comparison tables when appropriate.
  • Define technical terminology.
  • Identify the sources of statistics and research.
  • Use descriptive image alternative text.
  • Include relevant examples.
  • Break up long paragraphs.

For example, a page about choosing an SEO agency should not bury its recommendations inside several paragraphs of general marketing information.

Provide a clear explanation of what customers should evaluate, followed by detailed comparisons and supporting examples.

The goal is to make the information understandable without requiring readers to search through the entire page. Our free GEO Content Checker flags sections that are hard to extract.

8. Implement Relevant Structured Data and Schema Markup

Structured data provides additional context about the information published on your website.

Schema.org vocabulary can help search engines interpret business information, articles, people, products, and other entities.

Useful schema types include:

SchemaPurpose
OrganizationIdentifies an organization
LocalBusinessDescribes a local business
ArticleIdentifies editorial content
PersonDescribes an individual or author
ServiceDescribes a service
BreadcrumbListCommunicates website hierarchy
ProductDescribes a product
WebSiteDescribes a website

For example, a local service company could use appropriate LocalBusiness markup to communicate its business name, address, telephone number, website, and operating hours.

An educational article could use Article markup to identify its headline, author, publication date, and modification date.

Make sure structured data accurately reflects the visible content on the page.

Importantly, schema markup does not guarantee AI citations or enhanced search appearances. Google also states that there is no special schema requirement for appearing in AI Overviews or AI Mode.

Use structured data as part of a broader technical SEO strategy, not as a shortcut to AI visibility. You can generate and check markup with our AI Visibility and Schema Studio.

9. Earn Third-Party Mentions and Relevant Backlinks

Your website is not the only place where your brand's expertise should be visible.

Independent mentions help establish your business's broader digital presence.

Develop relationships with relevant websites and organizations that serve your industry or audience.

Potential opportunities include:

  • Industry publications.
  • Local newspapers.
  • Professional associations.
  • Business organizations.
  • Relevant podcasts.
  • Industry blogs.
  • Educational institutions.
  • Community organizations.
  • Reputable business directories.

Publish original research that others may reference. Contribute expert commentary to relevant publications. Participate in professional discussions and establish relationships with complementary businesses.

Focus on earning legitimate references from relevant sources.

Avoid purchasing low-quality links or creating artificial mentions simply to increase the number of websites referencing your brand.

The objective is to build a credible digital reputation. Our Directory Listing Kit covers the listings worth keeping accurate.

10. Optimize Your Website for Local AI Search

Local GEO is an important opportunity for businesses that serve specific geographic markets.

People increasingly ask AI platforms for recommendations based on location, services, and customer needs.

Examples include:

  • Who are the best roofing contractors in Dallas?
  • What are the top-rated HVAC companies in Plano?
  • Where can I find a reliable plumber in Frisco?
  • Which foundation repair companies serve North Texas?
  • Who offers emergency water damage restoration near me?

These queries represent valuable customer discovery opportunities.

To improve local visibility, focus on five areas.

Optimize your Google Business Profile

Maintain accurate business information, categories, hours, services, photographs, and contact details.

Encourage authentic customer reviews and respond professionally to customer feedback.

Create useful location-specific pages

Develop dedicated pages for the communities you genuinely serve.

For example:

  • Roofing Company in Dallas, Texas.
  • Roofing Contractor in Plano, Texas.
  • Roof Replacement in Frisco, Texas.
  • Hail Damage Roof Repair in McKinney, Texas.

Each page should contain useful information specific to that market.

Avoid creating hundreds of nearly identical pages that simply replace one city name with another.

Publish local industry expertise

Demonstrate knowledge of the geographic markets where you operate.

A Dallas roofing contractor could publish resources about:

  • How North Texas hailstorms affect roofing materials.
  • What homeowners should know about storm damage.
  • Roofing considerations for Texas weather.
  • How to evaluate roof replacement estimates.
  • What to expect during a roofing inspection.

Maintain consistent business listings

Ensure your business information is accurate across relevant directories, professional associations, and local business platforms.

Build local authority

Earn legitimate mentions from local publications, chambers of commerce, community organizations, and industry associations.

Local GEO combines traditional local SEO with content authority, business reputation, and geographic relevance. See our local search and AI visibility guide for how we apply this to service-area businesses.

11. Keep Content Accurate and Updated

Outdated information can reduce the usefulness and credibility of your website.

Review important content regularly to ensure that statistics, references, service descriptions, and recommendations remain accurate.

Update:

  • Industry statistics.
  • Product specifications.
  • Pricing information.
  • Technical recommendations.
  • Business details.
  • Regulations and requirements.
  • External references.
  • Outdated examples.

When making meaningful updates, reflect the actual changes in your publication or modification dates.

Do not simply change a date to make an article appear fresh.

The objective is to maintain useful, accurate resources that readers can trust. Our Content Decay report finds pages that declare no update date or have quietly gone stale.

12. Monitor AI Search Visibility and Citation Performance

GEO requires ongoing measurement.

Traditional SEO metrics such as keyword rankings, organic traffic, and backlinks remain valuable, but they do not fully explain how your business appears in AI-generated answers.

Develop a consistent process for tracking AI visibility.

Important metrics include:

MetricWhat to monitor
AI citation rateHow often your website is cited for relevant questions
Brand mention rateHow often your company is mentioned
AI share of voiceYour visibility compared with competitors
Citation sourcesWhich websites and pages are referenced
Brand accuracyWhether AI describes your business correctly
Referral trafficVisitors arriving from AI platforms
ConversionsLeads and sales associated with AI referrals

Create a repeatable prompt-testing process

Start with 20-50 questions your potential customers might ask.

For a local roofing company, examples could include:

  • Who are the best roofing companies in Dallas?
  • How much does roof replacement cost in North Texas?
  • Which roofing contractors specialize in hail damage?
  • What is the best roofing material for Texas weather?
  • Who provides emergency roof repair in Plano?

Test these questions across ChatGPT, Google AI experiences, Perplexity, Gemini, and other relevant platforms.

Document:

  1. Whether your business appears.
  2. Whether your website receives a citation.
  3. Which competitors are mentioned.
  4. Which third-party sources are referenced.
  5. Whether the business information is accurate.
  6. How results change over time.

Repeat the process periodically using consistent prompts.

AI answers can vary by location, query wording, personalization, and retrieval conditions. Avoid treating a single test as a definitive visibility measurement.

The objective is to identify patterns, understand competitive gaps, and determine which optimization activities are producing measurable improvements.

Example scorecard: for each prompt we record the platform, the date, whether the brand appears, whether the site is cited, the competitors named, and the sources referenced. A single prompt row is never treated as a result, which is why the same prompt set is re-run monthly.

Common GEO and AEO Mistakes to Avoid

As businesses begin investing in AI search optimization, several misconceptions can lead to wasted effort.

Here are some of the most common mistakes.

Publishing generic AI-generated content

Using AI to produce large volumes of repetitive articles does not establish expertise. Add original research, firsthand experience, expert insights, and meaningful value.

Ignoring traditional SEO

GEO and AEO complement traditional SEO. They do not eliminate the importance of crawlability, indexing, content quality, website authority, and internal linking. Our SEO, AEO, GEO, and AIO comparison explains where each discipline fits.

Overusing keywords

Repeatedly inserting GEO, AEO, ChatGPT, and AI search phrases does not make content more authoritative.

Creating thin location pages

Changing city names across nearly identical pages does not establish genuine geographic expertise.

Treating schema as a citation guarantee

Structured data helps communicate information but does not guarantee AI recommendations or citations.

Ignoring external brand authority

Your website should not be the only place where customers can find credible information about your business.

Failing to measure results

Without consistent testing, it is difficult to determine whether your business is becoming more visible in AI-generated responses.

Frequently Asked Questions About Getting Cited in AI Answers

How do I get my website cited by ChatGPT?

Publish authoritative, relevant content that directly answers customer questions. Maintain an accessible website, establish consistent brand information, earn credible third-party mentions, and build topical authority. ChatGPT citations are not guaranteed, and visibility can vary by query and retrieval conditions.

How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT?

Clearly communicate your services, expertise, location, and business identity across your website and relevant online platforms. Maintain accurate business profiles, develop useful service and location pages, earn authentic reviews, and build a credible reputation within your industry.

How long does it take to appear in AI search results?

There is no guaranteed timeline. Results depend on content quality, website accessibility, existing authority, competition, and how frequently AI systems retrieve relevant information. Some pages may become discoverable quickly, while building consistent brand visibility can require sustained effort.

Does Google ranking affect ChatGPT citations?

Google rankings and ChatGPT citations are different measurements. Strong traditional SEO can improve website discoverability and provides a useful foundation for AI search optimization. However, ranking highly in Google does not guarantee that ChatGPT will cite or recommend your website.

Does schema markup help with AI citations?

Structured data helps search engines interpret website information, including businesses, articles, products, and people. However, schema markup does not guarantee AI citations. It should accurately describe the visible information on your website and complement established SEO practices.

Are backlinks important for GEO?

Backlinks remain relevant to traditional SEO and website authority. GEO also benefits from broader brand credibility, relevant editorial mentions, original research, and independent references. Focus on earning links and mentions from reputable, relevant sources rather than pursuing link quantity.

Does publishing more content improve AI visibility?

Not necessarily. Publishing more content only helps when it provides meaningful value. Focus on comprehensive resources that answer relevant questions, demonstrate expertise, and support your website's overall subject-matter authority.

Can businesses guarantee AI citations?

No. AI platforms control their own search, retrieval, and recommendation systems. No SEO agency can legitimately guarantee that a business will appear in every relevant AI-generated response.

Should I optimize for ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews first?

Start with the platforms most relevant to your customers. Google AI experiences and ChatGPT are sensible starting points for many businesses. Then expand your monitoring to Perplexity, Gemini, and other platforms. Visibility on one platform does not guarantee visibility on another.

Final Thoughts: Build a Digital Presence AI Can Discover and Trust

Getting cited in AI answers requires more than optimizing individual web pages.

Businesses need to establish a connected digital presence that demonstrates expertise, communicates accurate information, builds brand authority, and answers the questions customers are asking.

GEO, AEO, and traditional SEO work together to support this objective.

Start by improving your existing content, building topical authority, strengthening your brand's online presence, and making your website technically accessible.

For local businesses, add geographic relevance through accurate business profiles, useful location-specific content, authentic customer reviews, and credible local references.

Most importantly, measure your visibility across AI platforms and continually improve the content and information sources that support your brand.

The goal isn't simply to rank in search results. It's to become a trusted source of information when customers ask AI platforms questions about your industry, products, services, and local market.
