Why are zero-click searches rising - and what can businesses do about it?

By AEOGEO Search

Quick answer

Zero-click searches - queries answered without any visit to a website - reached 68% of US Google searches in early 2026, up from 60.5% in 2024 (SparkToro). AI Overviews, featured snippets, and AI chatbots answer questions directly. Businesses adapt by optimizing for citations and brand visibility, not just clicks.

The trend is structural, not seasonal. When an AI Overview appears, the zero-click rate climbs to roughly 83% (Similarweb estimates). Add the billions of questions now asked directly to ChatGPT (2.5 billion prompts per day) and the picture is clear: a growing share of customer questions never produce a website visit at all.

That sounds alarming, but it changes the goal rather than eliminating it. If customers get answers without clicking, then brand presence inside the answer - being named, described accurately, and recommended - becomes the conversion point. Research from FirstPageSage (2026) also found visitors who do arrive from AI-influenced sessions convert better: visitor-to-lead rates of 2.6% vs. 1.7%, with longer sessions and more pages viewed.

Key takeaways

  • Treat brand mentions in answers as a top-of-funnel channel - measure it like one.
  • The clicks that remain are higher intent; make sure landing pages convert them.
  • Keep your facts consistent everywhere - AI answers are assembled from your whole digital footprint.
