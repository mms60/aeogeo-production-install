Common Questions About Our Services

Clear, direct answers to what businesses ask most before working with us.

How does AI Search Optimization differ from traditional SEO?

SEO focuses on ranking pages for clicks in search results. AI Search Optimization (GEO and AEO) structures your content so it gets directly cited and quoted inside AI-generated answers from Google Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and ChatGPT. AEOGEO Search combines both into one connected strategy.

What services does AEOGEO Search offer?

We offer 12 service options in six categories: monthly AI search optimization plans, workflow automation setup and support, content support, content repurposing, custom websites, and the knowledge base package. Our pricing page lists the current scope and prices.

How long does it take to see results?

Timelines vary by industry, competition, and your content's current state. Many businesses see visibility improvements within a few months. Because AI search results shift as platforms and sources change, optimization works best as an ongoing process rather than a one-time project.

Which AI platforms do you optimize for?

We optimize for the platforms your customers actually use: Google Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and ChatGPT, alongside traditional Google and Bing search. We prioritize the surfaces that reach your audience and monitor the rest as they grow.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. We work with small businesses, professional service providers, and growing brands. Whether you have an established website or are just building your online presence, we identify practical opportunities to improve how your business is discovered online.

What AI search optimization means

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of improving digital content so generative AI systems can discover, understand, and reference it in their answers. It focuses on clear explanations, relevant evidence, and consistent business information rather than only traditional search-result rankings.

Aggarwal et al. (2024), GEO: Generative Engine Optimization, ACM KDD 2024 reported improvements of up to 40% in visibility within generative engine responses in its experiments. Results varied across domains and methods. We use that research as background for agreed content and website improvements, not as a promise that an AI platform will cite your business.
