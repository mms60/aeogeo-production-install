How does AI Search Optimization differ from traditional SEO?

SEO works on ranking pages so people click them. AI Search Optimization (GEO and AEO) shapes your content so AI answers from Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and ChatGPT can quote it directly. We run both as one strategy.

What services does AEOGEO Search offer?

There are 12 service options across six categories: monthly AI search optimization plans, workflow automation setup and support, content support, content repurposing, custom websites and the knowledge base package. Current scope and prices sit on our pricing page.

How long does it take to see results?

It depends on your industry, your competition and where your content stands today. Many businesses notice movement within a few months. AI answers keep shifting as platforms and sources change, so steady work beats a one-off project.

Which AI platforms do you optimize for?

We work on the platforms your customers actually use: Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and ChatGPT, plus regular Google and Bing search. The surfaces that reach your audience come first, and we keep an eye on the others as they grow.

Yes. We work with small businesses, professional service providers and brands on the way up. Whether your site is established or you are building it now, we look for practical ways to make your business easier to find.

What AI search optimization means

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of improving digital content so generative AI systems can discover, understand, and reference it in their answers. It focuses on clear explanations, relevant evidence, and consistent business information rather than only traditional search-result rankings.

Aggarwal et al. (2024), GEO: Generative Engine Optimization, ACM KDD 2024 reported improvements of up to 40% in visibility within generative engine responses in its experiments. Results varied across domains and methods. We use that research as background for agreed content and website improvements, not as a promise that an AI platform will cite your business.
