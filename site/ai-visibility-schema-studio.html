AI Visibility & Schema Studio

A workspace that tests whether AI crawlers can actually read your website, audits the structured data you already publish, and drafts the JSON-LD your pages are missing.

You cannot tell whether a site is ready for AI answers by looking at it in a browser. A page can render perfectly and still hand a crawler a blocked path, an empty shell, or a description of your business that no machine can parse. The Studio requests your pages with representative crawler user agents and shows you what came back, page by page.

Every scan ends in something you can act on: the pages that failed and why, the structured data already on the page, and a JSON-LD draft you can copy, edit and pass to whoever maintains the site.

What the tool does

Three tabs, one website, and a report you keep. Each tab answers a different question about the same pages.

AI crawler audit

Submit a public URL and the Studio requests it the way crawler user agents do, testing up to 20 pages. You get the robots.txt rules that apply, sitemap discovery, HTTP status, whether the page text is in the HTML itself, noindex directives, page titles and whether JSON-LD is present.

Findings are stored with the scan, and a written summary of what the crawler saw can be generated alongside them.

Schema audit and creation

Read an existing page and see which structured data is already there and which properties are missing for the page type you choose. Then generate a draft JSON-LD block from details you supply.

Only enter facts you can verify. The result is a draft to review, not markup to publish unchecked.

Scan history

Scans, findings and generated schema are stored on your account. Come back to an earlier report, or run the same URL again after a fix and compare what changed.

Your records are private to your account, and each user only sees their own scans and schema.

What you get back

A list of findings with instructions, not a score with no next step.

In the report

  • Which pages were reachable and which returned an error or an empty shell
  • The robots.txt rules that stop a crawler from fetching a page
  • HTTP status, page title, noindex and text availability for every page tested
  • The JSON-LD already on the page, and the properties missing for that page type
  • A generated JSON-LD block you can copy out and edit

What to do with it

  • Fix access first. A blocking robots rule, or a page that only fills in through JavaScript, is why a crawler returns nothing. Copy changes cannot rescue that page.
  • Pass the schema draft to your developer, or paste it into your CMS once every fact in it is checked.
  • Re-run the same URL later to confirm the change reached the page.

The crawler is read-only. It does not edit robots.txt and does not install anything on your website.

Before you buy

I paid. Why does the workspace still ask me to subscribe?

The payment is matched to your account by email. Create your account, or sign in, with the exact address you used at Stripe, and the subscription is attached the first time you sign in. If you paid with a different address, contact us with the email and date you paid.

What do I need before my first scan?

A public URL for the crawler audit. For schema work you also need verified business or page details: official name, URL, description, address, phone, service details, author, product details, or the questions and answers for a FAQ page.

Does a clean scan prove AI crawlers can reach my site?

No. The audit uses representative crawler user agent strings. That is not proof that a real crawler's IP range passes your CDN or firewall. Use your server and CDN logs when you need that confirmation.

Will the Studio add the schema to my website?

No. It drafts the markup and you or your developer add it. The crawler is read-only and never edits your site or your robots.txt.

Does it check Google-Extended?

Google-Extended is a robots.txt control token, not a crawler that fetches pages, so it is not tested as a user agent. The audit does show the robots.txt rules that apply to the pages you test.

How many websites does one subscription cover?

One website per subscription, at $49 per month. Billing and cancellation are handled in the Stripe portal.
