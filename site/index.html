Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About AI Search Optimization, AEO & GEO

Straightforward answers to the questions business owners and marketers most frequently ask about Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), pricing, and our approach.

How does the AEOGEO Search Optimization process work?

We start with a complimentary 25-page DIY website GEO audit or a consultation where we evaluate how search engines and AI platforms understand your website currently. In case of a full-service plan or a project, we agree on the scope and price of the work before starting, and track our progress according to the monthly indicators and reporting included in the plan.

What is AI Search Optimization?

AI search optimization is an approach to optimizing your website, content, technical and structured data markup, and digital presence in order for AI and generative platforms (such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude) to discover, understand, and reference your business in generated answers. At AEOGEO Search, it combines four pillars: AI Optimization (AIO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), and Search Engine Optimization (SEO). Learn more at /resources/what-is-ai-search-optimization/.

How is AI search optimization different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO focuses on ranking web pages in search engine results so users click through to your site. AI search optimization builds on that foundation by structuring clear entity facts, direct answers, and supporting evidence so AI systems cite and recommend your business inside synthesized answers. Both work together. Strong SEO remains essential because many AI search tools retrieve from traditional web indexes. Compare all four pillars at /resources/seo-aeo-geo-aio-difference/.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), and how does it relate to AEO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) structures your website's facts, context, and authority signals so generative AI engines can verify what you do, who you serve, and why customers trust you when synthesizing multi-source recommendations. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) focuses on formatting clear, concise answers to specific buyer questions. Used together, AEO and GEO improve both passage extraction and brand citation readiness.

Will my business be guaranteed to show up in ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity?

No legitimate agency can guarantee placement or citations in AI-generated answers, because AI models generate dynamic responses that vary by user prompt, location, and model updates. However, you can meaningfully improve your readiness by ensuring AI crawlers can access your site, structuring clear answers and JSON-LD schema, and maintaining consistent business facts across your digital presence.

How long does it take to see progress from AI search optimization?

There is no fixed timeframe or guaranteed placement. Progress depends on your industry, competitive landscape, current website health, content depth, and how frequently search engines and AI platforms recrawl your domain. We agree the exact monthly or project deliverables upfront and track progress against the visibility and content indicators included in your plan.

Do I still need traditional SEO and Local SEO if I invest in AI search?

Yes. Traditional organic search and local map packs still drive the majority of high-intent website traffic, and AI search assistants frequently pull live citations from Google and Bing search indexes. Our AEOM framework combines AIO, GEO, AEO, and SEO so your business stays competitive across both traditional rankings and AI-generated recommendations.

The Free Website GEO Audit crawls up to 25 accessible pages of your website across 22 technical, content, AI-readiness, and trust checks (one free audit per verified account, no credit card required).
