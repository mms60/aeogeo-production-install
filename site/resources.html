What is AI search optimization?

AI search optimization is the practice of improving your website, content, and online presence so AI-powered platforms - Google Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and ChatGPT - can discover, understand, and potentially cite your business when they generate answers. It combines four disciplines: AIO, GEO, AEO, and traditional SEO.

Read the full article

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of structuring content so generative AI systems cite your brand inside their answers. The method was introduced by Aggarwal et al. in GEO: Generative Engine Optimization, published at ACM KDD 2024. Its experiments reported visibility improvements of up to 40%, with effects varying by method and domain. The initial preprint appeared in 2023; the publication year used here is 2024.

Read the full article

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

Answer Engine Optimization is the craft of structuring pages so answer engines - Google AI Overviews, featured snippets, voice assistants, and AI chatbots - can quickly understand, trust, and quote your business as the direct answer. It prizes clarity, extractable structure, and credible signals over traditional ranking tricks.

Read the full article

What is the difference between SEO, AEO, GEO, and AIO?

SEO optimizes pages to rank and earn clicks in traditional search results. AEO optimizes content to be quoted as the direct answer. GEO optimizes content to be cited specifically inside generative AI responses. AIO is the umbrella for making your whole business understandable and referenceable to AI systems. They are complementary layers of one strategy.

Read the full article

How are Google AI Overviews changing search in 2026?

Google AI Overviews - the AI-generated summaries at the top of search results - now reach more than 2.5 billion users monthly and appear on a large share of queries. They answer questions directly on the results page, which reduces clicks to websites: the top-ranking page loses roughly 58% of its clicks when an AI Overview appears.

Read the full article

Why are zero-click searches rising - and what can businesses do about it?

Zero-click searches - queries answered without any visit to a website - reached 68% of US Google searches in early 2026, up from 60.5% in 2024 (SparkToro). AI Overviews, featured snippets, and AI chatbots answer questions directly. Businesses adapt by optimizing for citations and brand visibility, not just clicks.

Read the full article

How do you get cited in AI answers from Google Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and ChatGPT?

To get cited in AI answers, publish question-led content with direct 40–60 word answers, back claims with named statistics and sources, use clear structure (headings, lists, tables, FAQ schema), keep information current and dated, and build brand authority across the web - because AI systems cite sources they can verify from multiple places.

Read the full article

Which AI search platforms should your business optimize for in 2026?

Start with Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT - they dominate AI discovery. AI Overviews reaches 2.5+ billion users monthly, and ChatGPT reaches 1.2 billion people weekly while sending roughly 87–95% of measurable AI referral traffic. Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, and Claude are meaningful secondary platforms worth monitoring as they grow.

Read the full article

What is AEOM - the AI Engine Optimization Method?

AEOM - the AI Engine Optimization Method - is AEOGEO Search's systematic framework for getting a business found, understood, and recommended in AI-powered search. It works in four steps - Discover, Optimize, Amplify, Monitor - and unifies AIO, GEO, AEO, and SEO into one repeatable process so every page and signal works together.

Read the full article

How do you measure AI search visibility and GEO performance?

Measure AI search visibility with five metrics: citation rate (how often your brand appears in AI answers to relevant prompts), AI share of voice versus competitors, brand-mention accuracy and sentiment, AI referral traffic in analytics, and conversions from AI-influenced sessions. Together these replace the old rankings-and-clicks dashboard.
