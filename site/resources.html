Frequently asked questions

What is AI search optimization?

AI search optimization is the process of improving a website and its wider digital presence so that information about a business is easier for AI-powered search and answer systems to discover, interpret, and potentially reference.

What is the difference between AEO and GEO?

Answer engine optimization focuses on making content useful and accessible for systems that provide direct answers. Generative engine optimization focuses on visibility within AI-generated responses. The approaches overlap and can form part of the same broader strategy.

Does traditional SEO still matter for AI search?

Yes. Technical accessibility, useful content, clear site structure, and a credible online presence remain important foundations. AI search optimization builds on many established SEO principles while addressing additional discovery experiences.

How can I improve my business's visibility in ChatGPT?

There is no guaranteed method of securing a mention in ChatGPT. Businesses can work on making their information accurate, useful, clearly structured, and consistently represented across their website and wider online presence.

How do I know whether my AI search strategy is working?

Monitor relevant AI search experiences, track brand mentions and referral activity where measurable, and review changes in organic visibility and engagement. Use consistent prompts and measurement methods so that comparisons are meaningful.
