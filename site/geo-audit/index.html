Know What's Holding Your Website Back

Your website could have excellent information and valuable content, but that doesn't necessarily mean AI-powered search systems can easily access, interpret, or extract it.

Our GEO Audit helps uncover potential obstacles, including:

  • Content that lacks clear, self-contained answers.
  • Weak heading structures and unclear page organization.
  • Missing or incomplete structured data.
  • AI and search crawler access restrictions.
  • Important pages missing from your sitemap.
  • Limited author, entity, and external reference signals.
  • Weak internal linking between related pages.
  • Content that lacks useful depth, context, or supporting details.

A Comprehensive GEO Audit Across 22 Readiness Dimensions

We evaluate your website using four core pillars that contribute to your overall GEO readiness score.

1 · 35% of your score

Content Quality

Make your content easier to understand, extract, and use.

AI search systems need clear, useful, well-structured information. We examine content characteristics that can make your pages more understandable and useful.

The audit evaluates:

  • Content readability.
  • Content depth and usefulness.
  • Standalone answer passages.
  • Supporting numerical details.
  • Content tone and clarity.

Your benefit: Identify pages where content needs more substance, clearer explanations, better answers, or stronger supporting information.

2 · 30% of your score

AI Readiness

Structure your website so important information is easier to interpret.

A well-organized website makes it easier to understand the relationships between topics and identify the most important information on a page.

The audit evaluates:

  • Heading hierarchy and organization.
  • Question-and-answer signals.
  • Image alternative text.
  • Semantic clarity.
  • Answer-oriented content structures.

Your benefit: Discover opportunities to improve how your information is organized and communicated.

3 · 15% of your score

Technical Foundation

Make sure crawlers can access and discover your important pages.

Even valuable content has limited potential if search crawlers cannot access or discover it.

The audit evaluates:

  • Search crawler permissions in robots.txt.
  • JSON-LD structured data.
  • Sitemap membership.
  • Initial HTML accessibility.
  • Declared content update dates.

Your benefit: Identify technical accessibility and discoverability issues that could make your website harder for search systems to process.

4 · 20% of your score

Authority & Trust

Strengthen the signals that help establish context and credibility.

Your website needs to communicate who you are, what you do, and how your content connects to relevant information.

The audit evaluates:

  • External source links.
  • Author declarations.
  • Entity references and sameAs declarations.
  • Internal linking structure.

Your benefit: Find opportunities to improve the connections, references, and contextual information surrounding your website content.

Our framework distinguishes measured HTML checks from editorial heuristics. Some dimensions may be unavailable or unscored. Authority and credentials are not independently verified. This is AEOGEO Search’s framework, informed by GEO research, not a standardized or university-certified scorecard. Two dimensions require additional evaluators and remain explicitly unscored in this build.

How is GEO different from regular SEO?

Traditional SEO and GEO overlap. GEO focuses specifically on helping generative search systems discover, interpret and potentially reference your content. This tool audits readiness signals; it does not establish your actual position in assistant answers.

Can you guarantee citations or traffic?

No. Search and assistant behavior varies by query, source selection and time. We provide diagnostics and practical recommendations, not guaranteed citations, rankings or conversion rates.

Does the free audit automatically start a subscription?

No. One free audit is available per verified account. You must explicitly choose and pay for a subscription through secure Stripe checkout.

What if my website has more pages?

Your selected allowance controls the scan. No automatic overage charges are enabled. If you need more than your included allowance, contact us for an agreed scope. Domains above 5,000 pages need a custom scope.

What does the simulator predict?

Only a hypothetical change to our scored checklist, assuming the selected issues are fully resolved. It does not predict traffic, revenue, actual AI ranking or citation lift.

Can I cancel?

Use Manage billing in your account to cancel future renewals and manage payment details. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your paid period, with no automatic refund. Annual prices are billed as one yearly payment; the 20% discount does not mean month-to-month billing.

Are these agency-service prices?

No. These are self-service software subscriptions. Done-for-you strategy and implementation remain on our separate agency pricing page.
